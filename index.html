About

I am a nonprofit professional with experience spanning development, institutional funding, program strategy, and communications within mission-driven organizations. I bring a proven ability to align funding with program outcomes, cultivate partnerships, manage cross-functional initiatives, and communicate impact clearly to stakeholders.

My background includes sustainability-focused work in higher education, leadership in nonprofit development, and community event production. I am most at home in roles that combine strategic planning with relationship building, and I am particularly drawn to organizations working toward equity, access, and community well-being.

Before working in nonprofits, I taught high school English, where I developed a pedagogy of cultural lineage: helping students see how the stories we tell today grow from the stories cultures have always told. That instinct for connecting ideas across contexts carries through in the way I approach fundraising, stakeholder communication, and program design.
